# SIGNATURE · Panaderia y Pasteleria El Trigal

## Brand truth
"El Trigal" means *the wheatfield*. This is a Mexican panaderia in City of Orange, NJ,
open from 6:00 AM, 4.9 stars. The pitch is the daily bake: conchas, pan dulce, bolillo,
and cakes for every occasion, hecho fresco cada manana.

## The ONE signature moment
**"El horno encendido" — the lit-oven hero.** A flour-cream stage holds a real tray of
conchas. Behind the wordmark, a warm ovenlight heat-haze rises continuously (CSS heat
shimmer, honey-glaze radial), and the wordmark "El Trigal" carries a slow golden glaze
sweep, as if light is moving across fresh-baked sugar. It is ambient, not scroll-coupled,
and it degrades to a static honey wordmark under prefers-reduced-motion.

Why it serves the business: a panaderia sells warmth and freshness from the oven at 6 AM.
The heat-haze + glaze sweep IS that pitch, not decoration. No competitor panaderia site
does this; the category default is a flat photo carousel.

## Lane (locked, honored)
- accent `#6F4E37` (toasted coffee-crust brown)
- display font **Boska** (Fontshare) for every heading + the wordmark
- layout: **stacked horizontal bands, alternating** (flour / paper / crust / dark)
- mood: **artisanal**, hand-made, warm ovenlight

## Distance vs recent builds
Warm flour-cream ground + coffee-brown + Boska serif + horizontal alternating bands is
unlike the dark-tech / blueprint / copper-thermal recents. No metal, no WebGL, no
dashboard. Photography-led for a food vertical (QUALITY_BAR: food = the food).

## Spanish-first copy
Site is in Spanish (the community served), with English mirror lines where it helps a
non-Spanish walk-in. No em dashes anywhere.
